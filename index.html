Sri Sakthi Amma

Sri Sakthi Amma was born on the 3rd of January 1976, to Shri. Nanda Gopal and Smt. Jyothiamma. It was evident from birth that this baby was divine and very special.

  About Sakthi Amma

Sri Narayani Peedam

Sri Sakthi Amma resides at Sri Narayani Peedam, a spiritual institution nestled at the foot of the sacred Kailasha Giri hills in Thirumalaikodi, near Vellore. Surrounded by scenic mountains, this holy sanctuary was blessed by centuries of intense penance by ancient sages. Today, Sri Narayani Peedam serves as a beacon of peace, devotion, and selfless service, welcoming millions of spiritual seekers from around the world.
