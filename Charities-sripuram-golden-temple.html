CHARITIES

Annadhaanam

Sponsoring Annadhaanam, the sacred act of providing free meals to the hungry and needy, brings profound spiritual blessings and nourishes the community.

  More
Green Sakthi

'Green Sakthi' has evolved into a significant environmental movement focusing on large-scale tree planting and nature education across Tamil Nadu.

  More
Gho Samrakshana

The Gho Samrakshana initiative was launched to rescue, protect, and care for aging cows, ensuring they live out their days in a peaceful and well-maintained sanctuary.

  More
ZERO-WASTE Management Programme

This award-winning Zero-Waste Management Programme features a large-scale bio-gas plant that uses animal waste to generate renewable energy and promote sustainable living.

  More
Gho Dhanam (Donation Of Cows)

The Gho Dhanam initiative empowers impoverished rural households by donating healthy cows, providing them with a sustainable source of income and nourishment.

  More
Gho Shala

The Sripuram Ghoshala provides cows with the highest standard of veterinary care, nutritious feed, and spacious shelters to ensure their absolute well-being.

  More
Mathru Ganga (Distribution of Drinking Water)

The Mathru Ganga project distributes thousands of liters of clean, safe drinking water daily to parched villages, offering life-saving relief to communities in need.

  More
Jyothi Swaroopini(Revival Of Temples)

The Jyothi Swaroopini project restores dilapidated rural temples, revitalizing spiritual institutions to create unity and preserve ancient cultural heritage.

  More
Sri Narayani Hospital and Research Centre

This world-class tertiary care facility is equipped with state-of-the-art medical technology to provide high-quality, compassionate healthcare to rural populations.

  More
Arogya - Health Care

The Arogya healthcare project provides comprehensive health screenings, primary care, and free life-saving heart surgeries to children in underserved communities.

  More
VidyaNethram (Education)

VidyaNethram removes financial barriers by providing essential school infrastructure, scholarships, and educational aids to encourage rural children to pursue higher education.

  More
Kalyani (Family Establishment)

The Kalyani project completely sponsors wedding costs, gold mangalsutras, and household essentials to help impoverished rural couples begin their family life.

  More
Sri Narayani School and College of Nursing

Established in 2006, this institution provides top-tier nursing education and extensive clinical training to aspiring young women from rural backgrounds.

  More
Blood Donation Programme

Through frequent spiritual donation camps, thousands of volunteers consistently provide life-saving blood to ensure hospital blood banks are always well-stocked.

  More
Sri Narayani Vidyashram

Sri Narayani Vidyashram imparts holistic education that blends modern academic excellence with deep-rooted traditional values to shape responsible global citizens.

  More
Coaching Classes

This initiative provides specialized tutoring and mentoring to help deserving youths clear competitive examinations and secure higher education or government jobs.

  More
Scholarship Programme

Hundreds of deserving students are awarded generous scholarships and financial assistance each year to continue their academic pursuits without financial stress.

  More
SAMMM Sakthi Amma Magalir Mempattu Maiyyam

SAMMM empowers underprivileged rural women by offering essential vocational training and employment opportunities to help them achieve financial independence.

  More
Tribal Community Education and Healthcare Programme

This dedicated programme provides extensive educational and healthcare assistance, including schools and mobile clinics, to uplift remote tribal communities.

  More
Dhaanam (Alms)

Vast quantities of food, clothing, and essential living supplies are distributed daily to ensure that no one in the surrounding region goes hungry or unclothed.

  More
Bhaktha Sabha

The Bhaktha Sabha is a dedicated volunteer group where thousands contribute their time and skills to support the Peedam's numerous charitable endeavors.

  More
Narayani Kala Peedam

This institution revives and promotes traditional Indian art, music, and dance through cultural festivals and rigorous training for young artists.

  More
Sri Narayani Vidyalaya

Sri Narayani Vidyalaya is committed to offering high-quality, values-based education to children from all socioeconomic backgrounds.

  More
Punarjanma (New Lease Of Life)

Punarjanma provides emotional counseling, financial assistance, and secure educational opportunities for the rehabilitation of prisoners and their families.

  More
Sri Sakthi Amma's Centre for Holistic Medicine

This sanctuary of wellness offers highly authentic Ayurveda and Siddha medical treatments to provide patients with natural, holistic healing therapies.

  More
